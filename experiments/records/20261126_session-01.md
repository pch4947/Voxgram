- `20261126_c01_p1_t15p_l0_02` was taken in portrait orientation, with the card

## Results

- **Commit used for the results:** 4f354d5 (summary in `outputs/eval/real-20261126/`)
- **Summary:**
  - All four photographs decode. Marker detection succeeds on all of them.
  - Full pipeline over the three `setup_ok = yes` photographs: gray MAE 9.68
    (2.28 dB), spectral error 2.43 dB, against 0.90 dB for the digital
    baseline. The reconstructed speech is intelligible on listening.
  - Calibration matters most: without it the gray MAE rises to 44.8 and the
    spectral error to 18.9 dB.
  - `--smooth 3` lowers the gray MAE to 8.79 and `--samples 3` to 9.29.
  - The remaining error has almost no bias in the mid-gray range; it is
    scattered cell by cell, which points at print dots and ink spread rather
    than at the tone mapping.
  - M3 gate: passed, for a card printed at about 1.86 times the specified size.
    The specified 0.29 mm cells are not yet tested.
